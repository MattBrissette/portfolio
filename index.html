Stryx 01 / 07

DTC, Shopify
UX Art Direction Design Lead Brand E-commerce

Stryx is a men’s grooming and skincare brand that was expanding across nationwide retail while growing its direct-to-consumer business. The brand needed a digital experience that could support that growth while making it easier for customers to discover, understand, and purchase its products.

I led the design, UX, and digital strategy for Stryx’s Shopify eCommerce experience. We evolved the visual identity into a more confident, premium system and reworked the customer journey across product discovery, education, bundling, and checkout. The experience included custom bundle configurators, clearer product storytelling, and a streamlined path to purchase, creating a more cohesive DTC experience from first visit to conversion.

RocketAir 02 / 07

Digital design & strategy agency
UX Art Direction Design Lead

A digital experience created in collaboration with RocketAir, blending creative strategy, thoughtful design, and high-impact visuals into a modern, engaging web experience.

Visuals were inspired by editorial/brutalist, bold typography as the visual anchor. A lo-fi space theme is contrasted against a bright neon yellow accent, set against a high-contrast, near-monochrome black-and-white base.

The whole experience was designed with motion in mind, with content sitting at the corners and edges rather than centered, leaving generous negative space to breathe.

SECLOCK 03 / 07

B2B, Industrial Supplies
UX Art Direction Design B2B E-commerce

I helped shape SECLOCK’s refreshed visual identity with a bold, contemporary color palette, flexible digital system, and typography inspired by West Berlin street signage. The result balanced the brand’s heritage with a more modern, recognizable presence.

I redesigned SECLOCK’s marketing website and helped bring the new brand to life across the digital experience. The messaging and content strategy focused on the customer journey, positioning SECLOCK as a leader, helper, and innovator while creating a stronger emotional connection with the brand.

I also redesigned the e-commerce experience around real B2B user needs. I overhauled the information architecture, restructured product-detail pages, and introduced predictive search, filtering, and contextual tutorials to make a complex product catalog easier to navigate and understand.

MCIM 04 / 07

B2B SaaS
UX Art Direction Design lead Brand

PE-backed tech company Fulcrum Collaborations was growing fast. As awareness expanded for both the corporate brand and its flagship SaaS product, MCIM (Mission Critical Information Management), it became clear that future growth would be amplified by the power of one cohesive brand. Fulcrum sought a unified, scalable brand – and a bold new website to define it.

I helped evolve MCIM’s existing brand into a more modern, confident, and technology-focused identity while preserving the recognition they had already built—creating the overall art direction, comprehensive brand guidelines, and custom iconography. For the website, I led the UX and wireframing process before designing the final experience, creating a clearer digital presence that communicates what MCIM does, who they serve, and why they’re a trusted leader in their industry.

OatFi 05 / 07

Fintech
UX Art Direction Design lead Brand

OatFi is a fintech lender focused on B2B payments, offering API infrastructure that lets platforms embed working capital tools directly into payment flows.

As OatFi exited stealth mode, I helped shape its positioning as a modern alternative to traditional lenders, contributing to a bold, dynamic brand built around black, white, and neon accents. I worked on the visual identity and modular design system, creating an adaptable language that reflected how OatFi’s API integrates across different platforms and industries.

For the website, I led the UX strategy, information architecture, and design—creating custom icons, custom illustrations, and brand guidelines to deliver a simple and engaging experience that clearly communicates OatFi’s product, differentiation, and value through bold visuals, natural language, and purposeful microinteractions.

Olive 07 / 07

B2C, E-Commerce
UX Art Direction Design Lead E-Commerce

Olive is a new way to shop online that replaces wasteful packaging with reusable, returnable totes while combining orders from different retailers into a single scheduled delivery.

I led the design of the product experience around this new shopping model, using a minimalist, utilitarian aesthetic to make the experience feel simple and intuitive. We prototyped and tested competing solutions with users, then built a cohesive design system across mobile and desktop to support consistency and scalability.

I supported the brand across every touchpoint, from the digital product and design system to email templates and launch creative, helping bring the new e-commerce experience to life.

About

I’m a Senior Digital Designer based in the Gatineau–Ottawa region with 10+ years of experience creating websites, digital products, brands, and interactive experiences across SaaS, eCommerce, and marketing.

My background sits somewhere between visual design, UX/UI, product design, and interaction. I enjoy taking complex ideas from early exploration through to polished digital experiences, balancing strong storytelling and visual craft with usability, performance, and business goals.

Over the years, I’ve worked with startups, ecommerce brands, and organizations across a wide range of industries, collaborating closely with clients, strategists, developers, and other designers. I’m comfortable leading the design process, presenting work, exploring different directions, and turning ideas and requirements into experiences that are ready to build.

I also integrate GenAI into my workflow to accelerate research, exploration, prototyping, and iteration. More recently, I’ve been exploring 3D, WebGL, and creative technologies as new ways to create more immersive and memorable digital experiences.

Always interested in creating work that is not only beautiful, but useful, thoughtful, and built to make an impact.
