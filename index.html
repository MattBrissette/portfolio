Stryx 01 / 07

DTC, Shopify
UX Art Direction Design Lead Brand

Stryx is a men’s grooming and skincare brand that was expanding across nationwide retail while growing its direct-to-consumer business. The brand needed a digital experience that could support that growth while making it easier for customers to discover, understand, and purchase its products.

I led the design, UX, and digital strategy for Stryx’s Shopify eCommerce experience. We evolved the visual identity into a more confident, premium system and reworked the customer journey across product discovery, education, bundling, and checkout. The experience included custom bundle configurators, clearer product storytelling, and a streamlined path to purchase, creating a more cohesive DTC experience from first visit to conversion.

MCIM 05 / 07

B2B SaaS
UX Art Direction Design lead Brand

PE-backed tech company Fulcrum Collaborations was growing fast. As awareness expanded for both the corporate brand and its flagship SaaS product, MCIM (Mission Critical Information Management), it became clear that future growth would be amplified by the power of one cohesive brand. Fulcrum sought a unified, scalable brand – and a bold new website to define it.

I helped evolve MCIM’s existing brand into a more modern, confident, and technology-focused identity while preserving the recognition they had already built—creating the overall art direction, comprehensive brand guidelines, and custom iconography. For the website, I led the UX and wireframing process before designing the final experience, creating a clearer digital presence that communicates what MCIM does, who they serve, and why they’re a trusted leader in their industry.

Global Expansion

Unified brand platform supporting growth across 90+ countries, helping strengthen market presence and accelerate demand generation. "Immediate improvement in leads and market reaction." — CMO

CargoSprint 06 / 07

B2B SaaS
UX Art Direction Design lead Brand

CargoSprint had outgrown its brand. As the company evolved from a specialized cargo payments provider into a broader logistics technology platform, its identity no longer reflected the scale of its offerings or its position within the industry.

I led the UX, information architecture, and visual design for CargoSprint’s new website, creating a clearer experience centered around speed, visibility, and operational efficiency. I also contributed to the evolution of the brand, creating comprehensive brand guidelines, photography art direction, and a refreshed visual language and design system that better showcased CargoSprint’s payment, appointment, and cargo visibility solutions while strengthening its positioning with global supply chain leaders.

Rapid Growth

In less than a year following launch, CargoSprint nearly tripled its headcount, completed another major acquisition, and established a digital platform built to support continued growth and expansion.

CaRMS 07 / 07

Healthcare / Digital Platform
UX Art Direction Design Lead Dashboard Platform

CaRMS' platform had grown increasingly complex, making it difficult to maintain, scale, and communicate across multiple stakeholders. The residency matching process relied on sophisticated algorithms and workflows, but the user experience and internal systems struggled to keep pace, creating friction for applicants, medical schools, and administrators.

We simplified the login and registration experience, reducing friction for new and returning users, while redesigning the program management workflow to make updating residency program information faster, more intuitive, and easier to maintain. The result was a more efficient platform for both applicants and administrators, built to support future growth and ongoing content management.
